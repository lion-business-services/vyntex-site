# Client case studies — publish only after sign-off

The SEO kit asks for 2–3 *real* stories. Source archives contain **no evidence** of client outcomes, approved testimonials, or permission to publish them. We have therefore created `/case-studies` with transparently labelled hypothetical solution examples and `noindex`, and intentionally left it out of `sitemap.xml` to prevent search engines from treating examples as proof of past work.

For a Lion Business Services case study, get written approval from the business owner and capture:

1. Company name and public URL approved for display; sector, initial constraints, services VYNTEX actually delivered, approximate project dates.
2. Authorized screenshots or live implementation links; what system was connected; what was specifically excluded.
3. Source-verified before/after metrics with dates and measurement methods (or qualitative results clearly attributed to the client), an approved quotation if any.
4. Client review and publication approval. Legal/privacy review for personal data.

Follow the same template for two additional clients. After approval, replace the example cards with verified stories, change page metadata to index, add `/case-studies` to sitemap and link the stories from homepage/navigation. **Do not invent business results, reviews or ratings.**
